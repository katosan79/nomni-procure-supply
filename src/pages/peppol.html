How it works

Three ways PEPPOL invoices reach Procure.

01

From Supply suppliers

Suppliers on Supply send PEPPOL invoices that land in the buyer's Procure invoice queue, matched to the PO and the delivery.

02

Through your accounting platform

Keep the access point you already use. Procure can pull PEPPOL bills from your accounting platform.

03

Alongside everything else

Photo, PDF and email invoices go through the same 3-way match, so your team works from one queue.

Common questions

Do I have to change my access point?

No. A business registers one access point for receiving, usually through its accounting platform. Procure works alongside it.

Can suppliers send PEPPOL invoices without an ERP?

Yes. Suppliers can issue PEPPOL e-invoices directly from Supply.

What happens to invoices that aren't on PEPPOL?

Snap, upload or email them. Procure reads them and runs the same 3-way match.
