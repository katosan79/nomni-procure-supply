How it works

Four steps, no new app for your customers.

Questions

Do my customers need to download anything?

No. They keep messaging your existing WhatsApp number.

Does it understand voice notes and Singlish?

That's what it's being built for. Voice notes are transcribed and matched to your catalogue, including local phrasing. We'll test it on real orders with beta suppliers before launch.

Will anything be sent without my approval?

No. Every draft waits for your approval until you choose to turn on autopilot for a specific customer.

Which systems will confirmed orders flow into?

Supply first. Pushing orders into AutoCount, SQL Account and Xero is planned.
