How it works

From outlet order to matched invoice.

Your central kitchen gets the same tools as a supplier on Supply, so internal transfers are tracked, costed and reconciled like any other order.

01

Outlets order

Outlets order from the CK in Procure, against their par levels.

02

Pick and pack

The CK team works from picking and packing slips on mobile.

03

Slip to invoice

A packed slip converts into an invoice in one step.

04

Pre-filled receiving

Each outlet's goods-received note is filled in from the CK invoice.
