Our AI principles

What if it gets it wrong? You stay in control.

01

Approve first

Agents draft. People decide. Thresholds set what needs a person.

02

Undo window

Every action can be reversed within a set window.

03

Shows its reasons

Every action carries a named actor and the reason it was taken.

04

Full audit log

See who or what changed anything, and when.

Structured data comes first: when a PEPPOL invoice exists, we use it instead of reading a photo.
